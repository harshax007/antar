# Catalyst SDK, i.e. with our deployment target. 0.6.11 is the newest release that
# works. Re-evaluate only when zcatalyst-sdk relaxes its typing-extensions pin.
# Verified 2026-07-18: pip check clean, full test suite green (550 passed).
