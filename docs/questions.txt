KSP INTELLIGENT CONVERSATIONAL CRIME DATABASE
HACKATHON TEST PROMPT SUITE — 165 QUESTIONS
Based on the supplied Karnataka Police FIR System ER Diagram

PURPOSE
Use these prompts to test a conversational AI connected to the FIR/case database. The prompts cover routine police queries, supervisory analysis, investigation support, data quality, cross-case analysis, public-safety planning, Kannada interaction, and security/governance challenges.

IMPORTANT TESTING NOTES
1. Replace placeholders such as [FIR number], [district], [police station], and [date range] with values that actually exist in the demo database.
2. The supplied ER diagram directly describes case/FIR identifiers and categories, registration and incident dates, information-received time, police station/unit, district/state, crime head/sub-head, gravity, case status, courts, legal acts/sections, complainants, victims, accused persons, arrest/surrender events, investigating officers, employee ranks/designations, GPS coordinates, brief facts, and chargesheet date/type.
3. The supplied ER diagram does NOT show dedicated tables for bank accounts or financial transactions, evidence/property seizure, witnesses, call-detail records, investigation diary actions, forensic results, population/economic denominators, judgments/convictions/acquittals, model audit logs, or authentication/authorization policies. For prompts involving these, the chatbot must use a connected authorized source or clearly state that the data is unavailable. Do not let it invent answers.
4. A name/age/gender similarity is not proof that two records refer to the same person. The schema shown has an AccusedMasterID within a case but does not show a verified cross-case person master key.
5. Treat an accused as an accused, not as convicted or guilty. Demographic correlations must not be presented as proof of individual risk or criminality.
6. For all analytical prompts, expect date ranges, applied filters, record counts, source FIR/case IDs where permitted, calculation definitions, data limitations, and role-appropriate redaction.
