# INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY  ACT 2000

Cited on **21,131 FIRs** (2.11% of the registry).

The registry stores the statute as free text in `ActSection` (for example `IPC 1860 U/s: 379`), so this concept is the ACT, parsed from everything before the `U/s:` marker. Section-level citation stays in the FIR row rather than being restated here, because the raw column holds **259 distinct act strings** and normalising sections would mean asserting a mapping the registry does not contain.
